# Licensing

Read this page before reusing Lumtail code, documentation or artwork.

Lumtail's original code, documentation and artwork use the [MIT license](LICENSE).
This clarifies the existing license; it does not transfer contributors' copyright
or replace the terms of third-party material.

| Material | License and notices |
| --- | --- |
| Original source code, tests, tooling and examples | [MIT](LICENSE); individual source headers identify the project attribution |
| Original documentation, guides and templates | [MIT](docs/LICENSE); Markdown files carry SPDX comments |
| Original SVG artwork and PNG exports in `assets/img/` | [MIT](assets/LICENSE); SVG files carry SPDX comments and PNG exports have adjacent `.license` files identifying their SVG source |
| Original terminal recordings and GIF exports in `assets/demo/` | [MIT](assets/LICENSE); adjacent `.license` files identify each recording and link GIF exports to their asciicast source |
| Incorporated third-party material and reproduced upstream notices | Its original terms, preserved in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and [LICENSES/](LICENSES/); see the [license review](docs/licenses.md) |

The copyright attribution is `Copyright (c) 2026 lumtail contributors`.
[contributors.md](contributors.md) records the original author.

## Reusing documentation and artwork

MIT permits copying, modification and commercial reuse subject to its notice
conditions. Preserve the applicable copyright notice and complete MIT text
with copies or substantial portions. The copies in `docs/LICENSE` and `assets/LICENSE` allow either
directory to travel with its license; they are identical to the root `LICENSE`.
For a standalone artwork export, include the applicable notice and license,
for example its `.license` companion and `assets/LICENSE`.

File notices use [SPDX license identifiers](https://spdx.org/licenses/MIT.html)
and [REUSE's comment and companion-file convention](https://reuse.software/spec-3.3/#comment-headers).

## Limits

Font-family names refer to installed system fonts.
Distributing font software requires its vendor's terms.
Copyright permission and trademark rights are separate.

Check the origin and applicable upstream notices before adding external
documents, images, fonts or datasets.
